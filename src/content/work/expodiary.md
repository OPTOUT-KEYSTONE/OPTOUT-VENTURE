Expodiary is currently being built at Optout Keystone.

We are exploring a simpler way to capture experiences and turn them into something useful over time. The product is still evolving, and much of the work right now is about understanding what should exist before deciding how it should work.

We build, break, test, and refine until the product starts making sense.
